The integrity charter

Trust is earned.

Rules of the vote

  • One accepted ballot per eligible, verified member.
  • Nominations determined by an independent committee.
  • Rules prohibit organizer edits or overrides; independent custody is required before launch.
  • Public register displays approved voter names, never their selections.
  • Ballot custody and tally reviewed by an independent administrator.
  • A participant may win multiple categories; tie rules published before voting.

Voting backend — draft implementation

The draft backend validates single-use member access codes and nominee IDs, rejects duplicate ballots, and publishes only approved display names. A signed Google Forms endpoint is implemented but has not been connected to a live form.

Google sign-in and a one-response setting alone cannot prove one person has only one account. Votes must remain closed until independent identity checks, access controls, audit logs, and end-to-end testing are complete.
