Privacy

LAST UPDATED · 2026-08-26

What this site collects

The public Answer North site collects no personal information, sets no tracking cookies, and runs no third-party analytics.

The fund terminal

If you create an account for the ASTRA-Q Paper Fund terminal, we store your email address and an authentication credential (handled by our authentication provider, Supabase, on our behalf). This is used solely to control access to the members-only terminal. Strategy requests you submit are stored with the email that submitted them.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share personal information.
  • We do not use advertising trackers.
  • We do not collect payment or financial account information — the Paper Fund involves no real capital.

Data removal

To have your account and associated data deleted, email hello@answernorth.com from the address in question.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it.
