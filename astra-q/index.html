01What ASTRA-Q is

ASTRA-Q is an experimental quantitative research system developed by Answer North. It is built around a simple rule: a strategy is not valuable because a model likes it. It becomes interesting only after the evidence has tried to break it.

Its governing sequence is an ordering constraint, not an optimization target:

Correct data → Reproducible experiments → Accurate factor replication → Adversarial validation → AI-assisted discovery → Forward paper evidence → Controlled live capital

The order matters. AI-assisted discovery does not come first. Returns do not come first. Live capital does not come first. The chassis has to earn the right to move forward.

02The operating loop

  • Observe — ingest point-in-time market and fundamental data with deterministic provenance and certified cutoffs.
  • Hypothesize — form explicit, preregistered signal ideas instead of retrofitting stories to returns.
  • Challenge — use placebos, external oracles, controls, and adversarial review to seek disconfirmation.
  • Validate — stress the surviving idea across time, exposures, costs, neutralization, and walk-forward tests.
  • Execute — move only qualified research into simulation and execution under explicit capital constraints.

The methodology behind this loop — experiment registration, evaluator freezing, evidence custody, and the capital-path gates — is documented as it is published. A dedicated ASTRA-Q methodology page is in preparation.

03Authority is staged

Research capability is not paper-trading authority, and paper authority is not live-capital authority. The current governance state:

RESEARCH — ACTIVE · Preregistered experiments over certified point-in-time data.
PAPER — ACTIVE · Frozen portfolios validated against live markets with zero real capital.
LIVE — NOT AUTHORIZED · Real capital requires an explicit governance gate that has deliberately not been opened.

The final line is not a roadmap delay disguised as governance. It is the governance state, and the system itself enforces it.
