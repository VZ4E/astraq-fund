About

AJ Gordon founded Answer North in 2026 to build infrastructure for governed autonomous intelligence — AI systems that must reason efficiently, produce evidence, and make consequential decisions.

AJ designs and operates the company's two research programs: ASTRA-Q, an evidence-first quantitative research system, and TLLTL, a model-agnostic layer for optimizing useful intelligence per unit of compute. The public record of that work is the Answer North research log, beginning with Research Note 001, Research that argues with itself.
